## What Are Local Citations?
A citation is any online mention of your business **Name, Address, and Phone Number (NAP)**. Search engines use citations to confirm that your business is real, physically located where you say it is, and trusted in the local community.

> [!WARNING]
> **Strict Quality Rule**: Only register on legitimate, moderated directories. Do **not** use automated software or pay cheap services that submit your business to hundreds of spam directories. Search engines actively penalize businesses with low-quality or inconsistent citations.
