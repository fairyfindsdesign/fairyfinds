3. Adding & Managing Products (Your Main Day-to-Day Task)

Whether you receive a fresh batch of Banarasi silk sarees, finish a custom velvet lehenga, or create ready-to-wear kurtis, adding them to your website takes less than 2 minutes.

Visual Diagram: Step-by-Step Product Creation Flow
1
Navigate to Products & Stock
Click "Products & Stock" in the sidebar. Click the bright pink "+ Add New Product" button at the top right.
2
Fill in Garment Essentials
Product Name: Use an attractive title (e.g. "Crimson Banarasi Katan Silk Saree").
Product Code (SKU): Unique code for the atelier (e.g. FF-RS-005). Auto-generated for you!
Price (₹): The retail price in Rupees (e.g. 18500). No commas or symbols needed.
Category & Collection: Select where this dress belongs (e.g. Category: Sarees; Collection: Festive Edit).
3
Fabric Composition & Care Guide (Important for Boutique Clients)
Shoppers love details! Specify the fabric (e.g. "100% Pure Mulberry Silk with Antique Gold Zari") and care instructions (e.g. "Dry clean only. Store wrapped in pure muslin cloth."). This builds trust and prestige.
4
Setting Size Variants & Stock Count
You can add multiple sizes (e.g., S: 2 in stock, M: 3 in stock, L: 0 in stock) or simply choose "Free Size (Saree)".
How Automatic "Sold Out" works: When the stock reaches 0, the website automatically shows an elegant "Sold Out / Inquire on WhatsApp" badge so you never disappoint a customer!
5
Uploading Photos & Featured Switch
Click or drag up to 6 high-res photos into the upload area. The first photo is your master display image.
Toggle "Feature on Homepage" to YES if you want this garment showcased on the main front page!

4. Categories vs. Curated Collections

To keep your boutique organized like a luxury Parisian or Milanese fashion house, garments are organized in two ways:

🏷️ Categories (Garment Types)

Permanent garment classifications that help shoppers filter your shop catalog:

  • Sarees: Silk, Organza, Banarasi, Linen.
  • Kurithis & Kurtas: Casual, Festive, Designer.
  • Blouses & Tops: Hand-embroidered bridal tops.
  • Dresses & Gowns: Western & fusion party wear.
Tip: You rarely need to change categories once set up.

✨ Collections (Curated Themes)

Seasonal or artistic themes that change throughout the year:

  • Onam Festive Heritage Edit: Kasavu & gold zari.
  • Emerald Velvet Bridal Story: Micro-velvet lehengas.
  • Pastel Symphony: Soft lavender & peach georgette.
  • Christian Wedding Atelier: Ivory & silver bespoke gowns.
Tip: Create new collections for festivals, seasons, or special drops!

5. Customizing the Homepage CMS (Without Coding)

The front page of your website is modular. You can change announcements, update banners, and rearrange sections in seconds from "Homepage CMS".

Visual Diagram: Modular Homepage Layout Sections

How to Reorder Sections

Want customer reviews higher on the page? Or want to put Featured Products above Collections?

  1. Go to Homepage CMS.
  2. Use the ↑ Up and ↓ Down arrow buttons next to any section.
  3. Click "Save Layout". Your live website updates immediately!

How to Hide / Show Sections

Don't have new reviews ready yet? Or want to temporarily hide the festival banner after Onam?

  • Toggle the green "Visible" switch to Hidden.
  • The section instantly disappears from the public website without deleting your data.
  • Switch it back on whenever you are ready!

6. Customer Reviews & Client Testimonials

Nothing converts hesitant online brides faster than seeing real, glowing reviews from previous customers in Kottayam, Kochi, Bangalore, Dubai, and the US!

Adding a New Client Review

  1. Click "Customer Reviews" in the sidebar.
  2. Click "+ Add Review".
  3. Enter the Client Name (e.g. "Dr. Meera N.").
  4. Enter their Location (e.g. "Kottayam, Kerala" or "Dubai, UAE").
  5. Set the Star Rating (typically 5 Stars).
  6. Paste their Feedback Quote (from WhatsApp or Instagram).
  7. Optional: Upload a photo of the client wearing the outfit!
  8. Click "Save Review".
Best Practices for Reviews:
  • Highlight custom fit ("The blouse fit like a glove on the first try!").
  • Mention speedy delivery ("Delivered to Bangalore in 2 days").
  • Include NRI experiences ("Ordered from the UK for my sister's engagement, flawless stitching!").
Did You Know? These reviews also generate Google Schema data automatically, helping your boutique display gold stars in search results!

8. Search Engines (Google, Bing) & AI Discovery (ChatGPT, Perplexity)

Your website has an advanced, built-in SEO & Discovery Control Panel specifically tuned for women's fashion in Kerala, NRI bridal shopping, and AI answer engines.

Visual Diagram: How Search Engines & AI Discover Your Boutique

Why Google Doesn't Update Instantly

Important Fact: When you update a title or description in your admin panel, it is instantly live on your website. However, Google Search only updates when its crawler (Googlebot) revisits your URL.

Google saves a cached copy of your site. It can take anywhere from 24 hours to a few days for Google to refresh its search snippet.

How to Fast-Track Google (24–48 Hours):
1. Open Google Search Console.
2. Paste your page URL in the top search bar.
3. Click "Request Indexing". Google puts you at the front of the queue!

AI Search (ChatGPT, Perplexity & Copilot)

Today, thousands of shoppers ask AI: "Where can I find a custom bridal blouse tailor in Kottayam?"

Your website is equipped with /llms.txt—an international AI standard that provides structured knowledge directly to ChatGPT, SearchGPT, Perplexity AI, Claude, and Apple Intelligence.

Bing & Copilot: Enter your Bing Webmaster Token in the Technical tab to ensure instant inclusion in Microsoft Copilot, Bing, and Yahoo!

9. Boutique Photography Guidelines (Maximize Sales)

You do not need an expensive photography studio! An iPhone or modern smartphone in good lighting will produce gorgeous luxury photos. Follow these 4 rules:

☀️
Natural Daylight

Shoot near large windows or an open doorway between 10 AM and 3 PM. Avoid yellow room bulbs.

🏛️
Clean Background

Use a neutral off-white, beige, or pale pastel wall. Keep the floor clean and uncluttered.

📱
Portrait (3:4 Ratio)

Always hold your phone vertically! Fashion garments look best in tall portrait orientation.

🔍
Detail Close-Up

Always include 1 close-up photo of the embroidery, zari weave, or neckline craftsmanship.

10. Frequently Asked Questions & Troubleshooting

Q1: A customer bought a saree in our Neendoor shop. How do I update the website?
Answer: Go to Products & Stock, click on the saree, reduce the stock count to 0 (or delete the item if it will never be restocked), and click "Update Product". The website will immediately update!
Q2: How do I offer a temporary holiday sale or discount announcement?
Answer: Go to Store Settings, change the Announcement Bar text to: "✨ Festive Season Special: Enjoy Complimentary Shipping on Orders Across Kerala!", and click Save. It appears across all pages instantly.
Q3: Can I manage the website while traveling using only my mobile phone?
Answer: Yes! The admin portal is 100% mobile-friendly. You can upload photos directly from your phone camera, adjust prices, edit stock, and toggle visibility from anywhere.
Q4: What should I do if a product photo appears sideways or blurry?
Answer: Make sure the photo was saved in standard orientation on your phone before uploading. For best results, use JPG or PNG images under 5MB each.
