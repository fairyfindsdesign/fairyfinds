## 3. Real Customer Review Strategy

> [!CAUTION]
> **Never purchase fake reviews or post reviews from staff accounts.** Google uses algorithmic detection to filter and penalize businesses with fake reviews.

### How to Collect Genuine 5-Star Reviews:
1. **Direct Post-Order Message**: After a customer receives their order and confirms satisfaction on WhatsApp, send a warm, personal message:
   > *"Thank you for choosing Fairy Finds Boutique! We hope you love your new outfit. As a local boutique, your feedback means everything to us. Would you take 30 seconds to share your experience on Google? [Your Google Review Shortlink]"*
2. **Review Shortlink**: Generate a direct review link in your Google Business Profile dashboard (**"Ask for reviews"** button) and save it as a WhatsApp quick reply.
3. **Always Respond to Reviews**: Reply professionally to every customer review within 48 hours. Mention the garment or service naturally (e.g., *"Thank you Ananya! It was a delight custom-stitching your bridal blouse."*).

## 4. Google Search Console & Webmaster Setup

Your website is technically configured with valid dynamic XML sitemaps, canonical tags, and structured data. Complete these steps to monitor Google's indexing:

1. **Access Search Console**: Visit [https://search.google.com/search-console](https://search.google.com/search-console).
2. **Add Property**: Add `https://fairyfindsboutique.store`.
3. **Verify Ownership**:
   - Recommended: DNS TXT record through your domain registrar.
   - Alternatively: Add the verification code in your website's Admin Portal under **Settings → SEO Management → Verification → Google Verification**.
4. **Submit Sitemap**:
   - In Search Console, go to **Sitemaps**.
   - Enter `sitemap.xml` (Full URL: `https://fairyfindsboutique.store/sitemap.xml`).
   - Click **Submit**.
5. **Inspect Key URLs**:
   - Use the URL Inspection tool to request indexing for:
     - `https://fairyfindsboutique.store/`
     - `https://fairyfindsboutique.store/shop`
     - `https://fairyfindsboutique.store/custom`
     - `https://fairyfindsboutique.store/about`
